# Daily Poké Catch

A daily Pokémon trivia game you play in the browser: **https://taziz05.github.io/daily-poke-catch/**

- **Daily Catch** – five questions a day (1 Easy, 1 Medium, 2 Hard, 1 Impossible), the same for everyone, new at midnight.
- **By Difficulty** – a separate five-question daily for each tier: Poké Ball (Easy), Great Ball (Medium), Ultra Ball (Hard) and Master Ball (Impossible).
- **Generations & Topics** – play any generation (I–IX) or a topic: Types & Matchups, Natures, Towns & Cities, Gyms & Badges, Elite Four & Champions, Professors, Game Characters, Villain Teams, the Anime, Movies, Music & Songs, Manga & Pokémon Adventures, Series Basics.
- **Who's That Pokémon?** – a Pokédex-style clue for every Pokémon from Bulbasaur (#0001) to Pecharunt (#1025).
- **Endless**, a searchable **Question Bank**, **Sync devices**, and a **Discord bot + Activity** (see `discord-bot/`).

Questions come from Bulbapedia, the official Pokédex on pokemon.com, the Pokémon Fandom wiki and Wikipedia, and every answer links its source. How the game is built and how to add questions: `build/README.md`.
