Privacy policy

Daily Poké Catch · Last updated November 21, 2026

Daily Poké Catch is an unofficial, free fan trivia game made by TAziz322. There are no accounts, no ads and nothing for sale. This page explains what the game stores and why.

On the website

  • Your results, streaks, Endless stats, secrets and achievements are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device unless you copy a Sync devices code yourself.
  • Visits are counted with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites.
  • The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google can see that your browser requested them.

In the Twitch extension

  • Twitch gives the extension an anonymous ID for you (not your username) and the channel's ID. The extension doesn't ask for your Twitch identity, so it never sees your username or email.
  • Your progress in the panel is saved only on your device.
  • When you answer a live play-along question, the game's server stores your anonymous ID, the channel, your answer, whether it was right and when. This is used to show the results and your score for the stream, and it's deleted after 30 days.
  • For each channel the server keeps the question that's currently running and a list of recent questions, so they don't repeat.

In Discord

  • The Discord bot stores your Discord user ID, your daily results and your name in each server where you play, for leaderboards and stats.
  • If you play the full game inside Discord (the Activity), your saved game progress is stored with your Discord user ID so it follows you between devices.

Usage counts

To see how many people play, each use of the Discord bot or the Twitch extensions (like playing /daily, opening the panel or answering a live question) is counted in GoatCounter. It's sent with a scrambled code made from your Discord or Twitch ID, so the same player is counted once, but the code can't be turned back into your ID. Live answers also send the viewer's country as reported by Cloudflare.

Who handles the data

The game runs on GitHub Pages (the website) and Cloudflare (the server and its database), and works inside Twitch and Discord under their own privacy policies. The game never sells or shares your information with anyone else.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13.

Questions or deletion

To ask a question or have your data deleted, open an issue at github.com/TAziz05/daily-poke-catch/issues. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
